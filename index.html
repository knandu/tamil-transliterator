Keyboard help & how it works

Space accepts the first choice even if it is still computing; keep typing. Punctuation accepts a visible choice and inserts that character. Click a choice to accept it without a separator.

Press 1–8 to accept that Tamil choice, or 0 to keep the displayed Roman spelling. ↑/↓ and click also select choices.

Enter accepts the first choice and adds a newline. After ↑/↓ or Ctrl+Space explores the word, Enter accepts the selected choice and adds a space instead.

Ctrl+Space reopens a Tamil word as editable Roman text. Esc restores the original Tamil during re-editing; otherwise it closes choices. F6 works while the textbox has focus.

The IndicXlit model runs locally in a browser worker. The first visit loads the model files; you can type while they load. Your text is not uploaded.
